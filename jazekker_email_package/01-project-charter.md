# JAZEKKER - Project Charter

## Mission

JAZEKKER is created as a public-oriented intelligence platform of a new generation. It helps people and organizations preserve clarity, understanding, and orientation in conditions of growing complexity.

JAZEKKER is not a conventional news website. It is an orientation-native civic intelligence system: infrastructure for intellectual orientation and a human-centered AI ecosystem.

## Main Goal

Create a sustainable environment for intellectual and civic orientation that:

- reduces cognitive overload;
- helps people understand change;
- supports conscious decision-making;
- strengthens human and organizational development;
- connects knowledge, signals, context, decisions, and trust.

## Strategic Vision

JAZEKKER should become an orientation-native civic intelligence system and public intelligence hub: a calm, explainable, trustworthy space where people and organizations understand AI, society, governance, infrastructure, and future systems.

## Project Formula

```text
Noise -> Signals -> Context -> Orientation -> Trust -> Coordination -> Clarity
```

## Core Principles

### Clarity Instead Of Chaos

The system helps users identify what matters, distinguish signal from noise, and understand context.

### Human Remains The Subject

Technology and AI support human orientation. They do not replace human thought, judgment, responsibility, or freedom of choice.

### Trust And Transparency

JAZEKKER must support explainability, transparent sources, responsible AI use, and respect for facts and context.

### Calm Intellectual Approach

The system must avoid manipulation, emotional overload, sensationalism, and dependency on endless content consumption.

### Coordination Instead Of Fragmentation

The project connects knowledge, reveals systemic processes, explains consequences, and builds coherent understanding.

## Scope Of Activity

JAZEKKER works at the intersection of:

- information and analytical systems;
- media and public orientation;
- AI and governance;
- educational and intellectual platforms;
- civic intelligence;
- coordination and explainability systems;
- research on human, social, and technological interaction.

## Role Of AI

AI is used as:

- a coordination tool;
- an intellectual assistant;
- a contextual intelligence system;
- a complexity-reduction mechanism.

AI must not:

- replace human thinking;
- create dependency;
- amplify informational chaos;
- manipulate attention.

## North Star

JAZEKKER does not measure success by:

- clicks;
- addictive engagement;
- content volume;
- attention retention;
- algorithmic dependency.

JAZEKKER measures success by:

- clarity;
- trust;
- integrity;
- ability to orient;
- quality of human decisions;
- sustainable coordination in conditions of complexity.
