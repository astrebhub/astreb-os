## Governance Placement

All future implementation must preserve:

- no uncontrolled publishing;
- human approval before publication;
- source tracking;
- content versioning;
- editorial categories;
- audit trail for AI-assisted content;
- separation between draft, reviewed, approved, scheduled, and published states.
