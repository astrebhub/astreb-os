# JAZEKKER - Agentic Strategic Media Operating System

## Updated Mission Frame

JAZEKKER is an orientation-native civic intelligence system and the public manifestation of an Agentic Strategic Media Operating System.

The system does not exist to generate more content. It exists to interpret reality under human strategic control.

## Main Architectural Shift

Earlier framing:

```text
AI Cabinet = separate governance project
```

Current framing:

```text
AI Cabinet = core of the agentic media operating system
```

JAZEKKER is not integrating AI into a website. JAZEKKER is turning AI Cabinet into an Agentic Media Runtime.

## Core Modules

### Signal Engine

Collects and normalizes signals from RSS, AI news, social feeds, manual ideas, articles, videos, regulation, GitHub or project updates, community signals, and institutional signals.

### Interpretation Engine

The differentiator is not summarization. It is systemic interpretation.

The engine asks:

- What matters?
- Why does it matter?
- What is signal and what is hype?
- What changes underneath?
- Who is affected?
- What should be understood next?

### Orientation Object Engine

The core content object is not an article. It is an Orientation Object.

Required fields:

- signal;
- context;
- noise level;
- impact horizon;
- who should care;
- systemic effects;
- next orientation step;
- sources;
- confidence;
- approval status.

### Narrative Engine

Forms angle, framing, positioning, strategic meaning, audience relevance, and calm interpretation.

### Content Transformation Engine

Transforms one approved insight into an article, briefing, explainer, LinkedIn post, Telegram post, newsletter item, carousel outline, or short video outline.

### Distribution Orchestrator

Manages the publishing queue and channel-specific outputs. No distribution bypasses approval.

### Strategic Memory

Stores themes, narratives, recurring patterns, audience reactions, successful and rejected framing, source reliability, ecosystem evolution, and editorial decisions.

## Governance Rules

- AI may draft, analyze, classify, interpret, transform, and suggest.
- AI may not publish directly.
- Human approval is required before publication.
- Sources must be tracked.
- AI-assisted content must have an audit trail.
- Draft, reviewed, approved, scheduled, and published states must be separated.
- Sensitive domains require governance review.
- Public trust overrides speed and volume.
