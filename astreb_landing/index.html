Positioning

Control systems for serious AI work.

ASTREB works on governance, orchestration, and operational intelligence for environments where AI output must be classified, routed, reviewed, audited, and held inside clear authority boundaries.

The focus is not model spectacle. The focus is the infrastructure that lets people use AI with traceability, quality control, risk awareness, and institutional memory.
