# JAZEKKER Source Policy

## Principle

Public trust depends on visible sources, clear uncertainty, and the separation of fact from interpretation.

## Source Requirements

Every Orientation Object must include at least one source record.

Every source record must include:

- title;
- URL or repository reference;
- source type;
- publisher;
- published date when available;
- last checked date;
- confidence level;
- verification status;
- notes on uncertainty.

## Source Types

```text
primary
official
expert
media
community
internal
unknown
```

## Publication Rule

Objects with placeholder URLs, unknown sources, or low confidence may remain as internal drafts, but they may not be published without review.

## Correction Rule

Corrections require:

- changed claim;
- original source;
- corrected source;
- rationale;
- approval record;
- audit record.
